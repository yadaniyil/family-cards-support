Family Cards

Know Your Family — support

Family Cards is a pass-the-phone question game for families. One phone, two to six players, no account, no internet needed.

Get in touch

Questions, bugs, a question card that felt off, or an idea for a deck — email yako@yako.dev. I read everything and usually reply within a couple of days.

The easiest way is Send feedback in the app's Settings (the gear on the home screen). It opens an email that already has your app version, iOS version and device model in it. If you write from somewhere else and are reporting a bug, please add those, plus what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game runs on your device. You can play on a plane, in a car, or anywhere with no signal. You only need a connection to buy a deck or restore your purchases.

How many people can play?

Two to six. One phone gets passed around — everyone plays from the same device.

How do I play?

Each card names one player, for example “What would Mom say is her dream vacation?” Everyone else guesses out loud. Mom then reveals her real answer and taps whoever guessed right. Each of them gets a point. Then the next card comes up.

If the guesses dry up, tap Stuck? Show 4 ideas on the card to get people talking again. Settings → How to play has the full rules.

What decks are there?

20 decks with 50 questions each:

  • All About Me (free)
  • Favorite Things
  • Would You Rather
  • Would You Rather: Family
  • Would You Rather: Big Choices
  • Would You Rather: Wild
  • Who's Most Likely To
  • Finish the Sentence
  • What If
  • Kids in Charge (only the kids answer)
  • Family History (only the grown-ups answer)
  • Life at Home
  • Food & Taste
  • Movies & TV
  • Music & Dancing
  • Screens & Games
  • Hobbies & Free Time
  • Animals & Pets
  • In the Car
  • Celebrations

All About Me is free for everyone. The other decks are one-time purchases. If you had Family Cards before version 1.2, Favorite Things (it used to be called Family Favorites) stays free for you.

What languages does it support?

29, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Ukrainian, Russian, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese. The app follows your iPhone's language. To play in another one, go to Settings → Language.

How do I add the widget?

The widget shows a new question about someone in your family every day. Tap it to start a game on that card.

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle. Tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Family Cards, pick a size, then tap Add Widget. It fits on the Lock Screen too: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Lock Screen, then tap the space under the clock. The same steps are in Settings → Home Screen widget.

What is Tonight's card, and how do I turn it off?

Tonight's card is an optional reminder at dinner time with one question for the table. It is off until you turn it on. To change the time or the days, or to turn it off, go to Settings → Tonight's card and switch off Dinner-time reminder.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything the game stores — player names, scores, which cards you have seen — lives only on your device and goes with it. Decks you bought are not lost: Restore purchases brings them back. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Is it OK for kids?

Yes. Every question was written for parents and kids to play together. There is nothing to skip past, no chat, no ads, and no way for anyone outside the room to reach you through the game. Purchases go through Apple, so Ask to Buy works as usual.

Buying and restoring decks

How does buying a deck work?

You pay once and the deck is yours. There is no subscription. Apple handles the payment. If you use Family Sharing, everyone in your family group gets the decks you buy.

I got a new phone, or a deck is locked again

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases. You can also tap Restore purchases on any locked deck. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought the deck with.

Still locked? Tap Send feedback in Settings. The email it opens has your purchase ID in it, so I can check what went wrong.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and pick the purchase. I can't give refunds myself, but I'm happy to help if something didn't work.
