Family Cards

Guess & Reveal — support

Family Cards is a pass-the-phone question game for families. One phone, two to six players, no account, no internet needed.

Get in touch

Questions, bugs, a question card that felt off, or an idea for a deck — email yako@yako.dev. I read everything and usually reply within a couple of days.

If you are reporting a bug, it helps a lot to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole game runs on your device. You can play on a plane, in a car, or anywhere with no signal.

How many people can play?

Two to six. One phone gets passed around — everyone plays from the same device.

How do I play?

Each card names one player, for example “What would Mom say is her dream vacation?” Everyone else guesses out loud. Mom then reveals her real answer and taps whoever guessed right. Each of them gets a point. Then the next card comes up.

If the guesses dry up, tap the hint on the card and it offers four ideas to get people talking again.

What languages does it support?

29, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Ukrainian, Russian, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese. The app follows your iPhone's language automatically.

When are the other decks coming?

Road Trip and When You Were My Age are in the works. They will show up as an update — you will not need to reinstall anything.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything the game stores — player names, scores, which cards you have seen — lives only on your device and goes with it. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Is it OK for kids?

Yes. Every question was written for parents and kids to play together. There is nothing to skip past, no chat, no ads, and no way for anyone outside the room to reach you through the game.
